EXPEDITION ROUTE & SCHEDULE

10-Day K2 Basecamp Expedition Itinerary

Explore the step-by-step breakdown of altitude progression, acclimatization days, and key trekking landmarks.

Fly into Skardu airport with stunning views of Nanga Parbat. Full equipment check, gear inspection, and official expedition safety briefing with certified Karakoram guides.

  • Stay: Mountain Lodge Skardu
  • Trek Distance: Rest / Preparation Day
  • Highlights: Shangrila Resort & Kachura Lake Visit

Thrilling 4x4 Jeep ride along the Shigar River valley to Askole, the final inhabited village before entering the deep Karakoram wilderness.

  • Stay: Riverside Expedition Camp
  • Drive Time: 6 - 7 Hours
  • Highlights: Shigar Cold Desert & Braldu Gorge

First official foot-trekking day! Cross the Braldu River bridge and march through rugged mountain trails surrounded by towering granite spires.

  • Stay: Tented Camp
  • Trek Distance: 20 km (6 Hours)
  • Highlights: Bakhor Das Peak Views

Critical rest and body acclimatization day to prevent Acute Mountain Sickness (AMS). Short walk up Paiju Peak ridge for active adaptation.

  • Stay: Paiju Camp Base
  • Activity: Acclimatization Hike & Health Checks
  • Highlights: First Sight of Baltoro Glacier snout

Reach Concordia, where the Baltoro Glacier meets the Godwin-Austen Glacier. Surrounded by 4 of the world’s 14 highest 8,000m peaks.

  • Stay: Glacier High Camp
  • Trek Distance: 18 km (7 Hours)
  • Highlights: 360° Views of K2, Broad Peak & Gasherbrum
