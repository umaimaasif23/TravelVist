🚨 Emergency & Safety Contacts

Quick access to rescue hotlines, mountain safety protocols, and medical advice.

📞

Rescue Hotlines

  • Askari Aviation (Helicopter): Call 1122
  • Mountain Rescue Base: +92-5111
  • Gilgit Disaster Relief: Call 1122

⚠️ Keep sat-phone or offline emergency numbers saved during treks.

🩺

AMS & Medical First-Aid

  • ✔ Golden Rule: Stop ascending if headache or nausea persists.
  • ✔ Immediate Action: Descend 500m-1000m down if symptoms worsen.
  • ✔ Hydration: Drink 4+ Liters of warm fluids with ORS daily.
Medical Rule: Descend Early
🧭

Survival & Navigation

  • ▸ Never trek alone after 4:00 PM in high passes.
  • ▸ Keep battery packs warm inside sleeping bags at night.
  • ▸ Mark offline GPS checkpoints on Maps.me / AllTrails.
